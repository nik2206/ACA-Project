01 · The question

Energy is now a first-class cost: battery, heat, power limits

On a modern multicore CPU, does making code faster also make it more energy-efficient?

Tool: the energy roofline model of Choi, Bedard, Fowler & Vuduc (IPDPS 2013) and its 2014 extensions.

Research question
02 · BackgroundTime

The roofline in time

Algorithm: W flops, Q DRAM bytes, intensity I = W/Q (flop/B).

T = max(W·τflop, Q·τmem)

Flops and bytes overlap: the slower one wins. The corner at Bτ = τmem/τflop separates memory-bound from compute-bound.

Measured roofline, turbo on: peak 362 GFLOP/s, 40 GB/s → Bτ = 9.03

Williams et al. 2009 · Choi et al. 2013
02 · BackgroundEnergy

Energy does not overlap

Paper 1, §II
E = W·εflop + Q·εmem + π0·Tevery flop and every byte is paid for; constant power π0 is paid for the whole run
Bε = εmem / εflopenergy balance, in flop/B (paper's Table I says "flops per Joule": a typo)
ε0 = π0·τflopconstant energy charged to each flop at peak rate; ηflop = εflop/(εflop+ε0)
B̂ε(I) = η·Bε + (1−η)·max(0, Bτ−I)effective energy balance: where the arch line reaches half its peak
Choi, Bedard, Fowler, Vuduc · IPDPS 2013
02 · BackgroundArch line

Sum, not max: the arch line

  • B̂ε > Bτ: balance gap. Code can be compute-bound in time yet memory-bound in energy.
  • Bτ > B̂ε: race-to-halt. Fast code is already within 2× of the best efficiency.
  • Large π0 lowers B̂ε and favours racing.

Measured arch line, turbo on: peak 6.1 GFLOP/J

04 · ResultsPowerline

The power cap

  • Uncapped model wants 85 W at Bτ; the laptop never drew above 67 W.
  • Capped model (Δπ = 44 W): power error max 20% → 5.5%.
  • It over-predicts the slowdown: the CPU drops frequency and voltage, so each op gets cheaper.

06 · Critique & conclusion

Strengths

  • 5 numbers, < 2% energy error
  • Algorithm-centred (W, Q, I)
  • Explains race-to-halt via π0
  • Extends cleanly: caps, caches

Weaknesses (our evidence)

  • Constants change 3× with DVFS
  • Cap and throttling; best-case overlap
  • Q counts DRAM only (matmul 1.4–2.4× off)
  • No P/E cores, SMT or contention
  • Package-only energy: turbo-off verdict DRAM-sensitive

Faster ⇒ greener at a fixed clock, but the fastest clock is not the greenest.
